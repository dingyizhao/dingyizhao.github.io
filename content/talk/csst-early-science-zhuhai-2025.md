## Event
CSST Early Science Discussion Meeting, Zhuhai, China.

## Talk Summary
Proposed AI-driven reconstruction of galaxy kinematics and mass distributions
from CSST wide-field multi-band imaging, with a MaNGA-calibrated observing plan
and application to million-galaxy samples from the CSST main survey.
