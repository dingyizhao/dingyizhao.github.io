## Event
Dynamic Cosmic Structures 2026, Hangzhou, China.

## Talk Summary
Presented the reassessment of central-galaxy quenching and one-halo galactic
conformity using halo-mass estimates consistent with independent weak-lensing
constraints, including the null conformity result and the stronger correlate of
quenching in the From Halos to Galaxies series.
