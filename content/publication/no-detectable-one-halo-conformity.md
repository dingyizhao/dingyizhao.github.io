## Abstract
Using three halo-mass estimates for SDSS galaxy groups, this Letter shows that
the strong one-halo galactic conformity signal obtained with conventional
abundance-matching halo masses is not reproduced with machine-learning halo
masses consistent with independent weak-lensing constraints, suggesting that the
earlier signal was largely driven by halo-mass estimation biases.
