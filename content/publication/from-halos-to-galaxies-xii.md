## Abstract
This work reassesses the relative roles of halo mass and central-region
properties in central-galaxy quenching using halo-mass estimates whose
stellar-to-halo mass relations are consistent with independent weak-lensing
measurements. With these estimates, halo mass becomes the stronger correlate of
quenching, whereas conventional abundance-matching halo masses recover the
previously reported stronger dependence on central velocity dispersion.
